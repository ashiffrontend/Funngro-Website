Funngro is India's leading youth freelancing and experiential learning ecosystem. It connects ambitious teenagers and college students (aged 14–25) with verified companies for digital projects such as web development, graphic design, content writing, social media, and video editing. Students work on flexible micro-tasks, receive mentorship, and earn direct income safely.

Any student aged 14 to 25 with an eagerness to learn and basic digital skills can join Funngro for free. Whether you attend school, college, or an educational training program, our community welcomes beginners ready to build practical portfolios.

Yes. For students under 18, parental or guardian consent is mandatory and strictly verified. During signup, parents receive a notification explaining the platform, safety policies, and payment terms, ensuring full visibility into their child's activities and earnings.

All client payments are held securely in milestone escrow before project work begins. Once you deliver the completed assignment and the company approves it, funds are released directly to your verified bank account or UPI ID with zero delays or hidden fees.

All Funngro micro-gigs are completely self-scheduled and remote. You decide how many hours you wish to dedicate each week, and you can easily pause accepting new work whenever you need to prepare for school exams or family events.

No prior professional experience is required! Funngro offers starter micro-tasks, sample brief templates, and community guides to help first-timers complete their initial project successfully and build immediate confidence.

Escrow protects everyone: students have 100% certainty that project funds are already deposited and will be paid upon completion, while businesses have certainty that payments will only be released once the submitted deliverable meets the agreed project brief.
